This is **much better** than the previous version. 👏

The empty space problem is mostly solved. The side panels make the desktop version feel like a real game homepage rather than a mobile screen stretched across a monitor.

### What's working well

✅ Stats panel on the left
✅ World progress card
✅ Recent badges section
✅ Worlds panel on the right
✅ Special modes grouped together
✅ Clean center focus on the Play button
✅ Mobile-first design can still collapse nicely

### What still feels weak

#### 1. The center hero section is still a bit static

The cat and mouse are very small.

Consider:

* Larger animated cat and mouse
* Cat chasing mouse across the center
* Mouse occasionally dropping cheese
* Small paw trail animation

#### 2. Side cards look disconnected

All cards have the same visual weight.

Try:

* Make "Worlds" the primary card
* Make "Recent Badges" smaller
* Highlight latest unlocked badge

#### 3. Missing a "Wow" factor

Many successful web games have a strong hero area.

Example:

```text
CAT CHASE

🐱 chasing 🐭

30 Levels • 3 Worlds • 24 Achievements

[ PLAY ]
```

with subtle animation.

#### 4. Add a Featured Achievement card

Instead of:

```text
Recent Badges
```

show:

```text
🏆 Latest Achievement

Speed Demon
Complete a level with 80% time remaining
```

This gives players a goal.

#### 5. Add Progress Percentage

Current:

```text
Sunny Fields 3/10
```

Better:

```text
Overall Progress
23%
██████░░░░░░░░
```

Players love completion tracking.

#### 6. Add a Daily Goal

Something like:

```text
🎯 Today's Goal

Catch 25 mice

12 / 25
```

Very effective for retention.

---

### My rating

**Old version:** 6.5/10

**Current version:** 8.5/10

**With hero animation + featured achievement + overall progress:** 9.2/10

I would not add many more panels. The screen is close to becoming crowded. Focus on making the **center area more lively and animated** rather than adding more boxes. That's where you'll get the biggest improvement now.
