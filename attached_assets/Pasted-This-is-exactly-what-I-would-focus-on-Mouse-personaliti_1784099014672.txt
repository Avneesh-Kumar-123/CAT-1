This is exactly what I would focus on. **Mouse personalities** give you the biggest improvement for the least amount of development work because they make every level feel different.

Here are **30 mouse personality ideas**, ranked from easiest to hardest.

---

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Tier 1 (Build these first)

## 😴 1. Sleepy Mouse

* Sleeps for 3–5 seconds.
* Wakes up if the cat gets close.
* Then runs away.

---

## 🥷 2. Ninja Mouse

* Every 4 seconds dashes quickly.
* Leaves a smoke effect.

---

## 🧀 3. Greedy Mouse

* Always runs toward nearby cheese.
* Easier to lure into traps.

---

## 😨 4. Scared Mouse

* Panics when the cat is close.
* Changes direction randomly.

---

## 🤪 5. Confused Mouse

* Every few seconds chooses the wrong direction.
* Runs into walls sometimes.

---

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Tier 2

## ⚡ 6. Speed Burst Mouse

* Normally slow.
* Gets a speed boost when the cat is nearby.

---

## 🐢 7. Lazy Mouse

* Walks instead of runs.
* Occasionally stops to rest.

---

## 🌀 8. Dizzy Mouse

* Walks in circles.
* Hard to predict.

---

## 🕳 9. Tunnel Mouse

* Uses tunnels to teleport.

---

## 👻 10. Ghost Mouse

* Can pass through special walls.

---

If you catch it while eating:

```
Perfect Catch

+50 Coins
```

---

### 💤 Yawning Mouse

Yawns before running.

Gives the player a brief reaction window.

---

### 🎩 Rich Mouse

Carries a money bag.

Drops extra coins if caught.

---

### 🎵 Dancing Mouse

Stops to dance every few seconds.

Players love quirky animations.

# My favorite (if I could only build one)

## 🎭 Trickster Mouse

When you get close:

```
🐭
↓

🐭 🐭 🐭

Three identical mice appear.

Only one is real.

The fake mice disappear after a few seconds.
```

Players remember moments like this and tell friends about them. It's memorable without requiring a whole new game mode.
